# Validation against 2026 published bills

Generated by `scripts/validate_history.py`. Each 2026 monthly edition of the Florida Municipal Electric Association's Electric Bill Comparison publishes residential bills at 1,000, 1,200 and 2,500 kWh. Each published bill is recalculated with the calculator, using the rate version in effect that month (`data/history/`), and compared with the published total (before gross receipts tax and franchise fees).

A check counts only when a primary source (tariff sheet, utility rate insert, PSC order or PSC billing table) documents the rate version for that month. FMEA rounds each bill component to the cent before adding them, so differences of a few cents are reported as *within rounding*.

## Result

- **117 documented checks**: 74 exact, 37 within rounding (largest difference 2 cents), 6 mismatches. 111 of 117 match.
- 18 further checks use months where the rate change date is inferred: 14 exact, 4 within rounding, 0 mismatches. These are not counted.
- Duke's September bills confirm that its second 2026 base-rate change, filed as effective September 1, was not yet in FMEA's or the PSC's September figures; both show June base rates with the new asset securitization charge.
- Not checked: the three municipal utilities (JEA, Tallahassee, OUC), whose past rate sheets were not available, and 2025 editions.
